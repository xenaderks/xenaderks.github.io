Beasts Awakening

Beast Awakening was originally a school project— A technical challenge centering the concept of modulation. I love contrasting a restful start with sections that feel huge and imposing; like ancient beasts marching together after a millenia of slumber. That escalation, combined with the fact that this piece is where I stopped worrying and learned to love clarinet, turned Beasts Awakening into a piece I view as significantly demonstrative of my current style of composition.



I wanted to give some extra insight into the details of the composition. For that purpose, you can check out an updated version of the sheet music in this pdf!
