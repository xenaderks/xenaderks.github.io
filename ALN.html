Alone

I created the concept for Alone in about 2 days, then shelved it as I had more pressing matters to attend to. Only recently did I pick it back up and finish it as part of the research I did for my thesis (linked below, if you're interested.)

Among other things, this was the first time I designed a save/load system. Previous projects never reached a scope where saving and loading was needed, and it was kind of a weird gap in my skillset as a programmer. Finally engaging this challenge was fun and tricky. Obviously just pushing a dict to a file and retrieiving it isn't super challenging, but making that process as efficient as I could with no experience in this area was a nice way to explore how these things are usually done.

The idea first came about when a friendship ended rather abruptly. It made me feel kind of paralyzed, like I had fewer ways to move around in the world... The end result is quite cheerful when put next to the title, but I think the way the music adapts helps to get the tone to where I wanted it.



I created all music and SFX for Alone, as well as the graphics and code. The way it's implemented makes it hard to let you hear here, so I heartily invite you to give it a playthrough! I'm really proud of it.
