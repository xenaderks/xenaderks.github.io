Unamed Ghost Game

In my third year at the HKU, I was asked to contribute to a rhythm game where getting a streak of perfect hits would allow you to break out and hunt the ghost that had been accosting you across the screen. Think pac-man meets DDR. Though it was a pleasure to work on, friction over creative direction in other parts of the team ended up breaking off the project before its completion. These tracks would've been used in the more straight-forward DDR-ey parts of the game. The first would've been an early level, the second a later one with trickier rhythms.
