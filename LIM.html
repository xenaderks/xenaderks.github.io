Limbus

LIMBUS was part of a cooperation with theater students at the Glasgow Royal Conservatoire. The protagonist experiences multiple steps in the story that leads to her eventual great sin, fall from grace, and damnation. Limbus is a thrilling, personal, sometimes uncomfortably close piece which is, of course, best experienced in person.

By the time the music was to be added to Limbus, the script was already finalized and there was a clear idea on the direction the writers and actors had in mind. Luckily, the music team was given a loose leash to play by, which gave us the opportunity to make our part of the play into something special. I played none of the music, but composed most of it. A big part, however, was going into the studio with a group of talented musicians and 'finding' the composition as we went. I recorded and directed the musicians in the studio, then produced the piece and helped integrate it into the play once we arrived on-scene.

I didn't manage to reach the people working with me, but once I get a hold of them I'll be adding links to their socials/websites. This was obviously an incredibly cooperative project.
