Zorpon

Zorpon was the first in a series of games that would eventually lead to the founding of my company. An unidentifyable entity has eluded you for the longest time. Your goal is to play through several minigames to find the true identity of the all-mighty Zorpon—preferably without losing your mind!

try ZORPON for yourself

I provided three tracks for Zorpon: the 'sandcastle' level, the 'question' theme, and the main menu music, which can be heard in the trailer. In the question frame you have the opportunity to fill in what you think Zorpon is. It's a slightly offputting space where all you can do is type in possible answers (there's a right answer, I promise.) Contrarily, the sandcastle level is one of the calmer levels. You simply get to build a pretty sandcastle as the tide comes rolling in.
